About

Louis Cluzel is a light designer and scenographer, working where light meets material and architecture.

Trained as an engineer at CentraleSupélec and as a designer at Politecnico di Milano, he works both sides of every piece: dreaming it up, then making it real. What is drawn on paper has to stand, move and switch on.

For ten years he has been lighting electronic music. After Novelty Group, he has built long collaborations with festivals such as Château Perché and Kamiwaza in France, Bucht der Träumer in Germany, Komorebi in Italy and Waking Life in Portugal, and as far as Japan.

His work is neither plain lighting engineering nor decoration. It lives on the threshold between them: where a material catches light, where a structure becomes the source, where staging and light become a single gesture.

He also makes objects that sit between the physical and the digital.
